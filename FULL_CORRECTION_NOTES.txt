MusicMax Full Correction - Queue / Auto-Play

1. Album songs
   - Every album song passes the complete album queue.
   - Next/Previous moves through all album tracks.

2. Artist songs
   - The legacy song={list} pattern is handled correctly.
   - Next/Previous moves through the complete artist Top Songs list.

3. Playlist songs
   - PlaylistDetails passes its complete songs array.
   - Next/Previous moves through all playlist tracks.

4. Search / SongGrid
   - SongGrid accepts songs, songList, or song array as the queue.
   - The clicked track remains inside the correct queue.

5. Single-song playback
   - A standalone song creates a one-song queue.
   - It no longer inherits an old album/playlist queue.
   - At the natural end, the player stays stopped because there is no next track.

6. Auto-play
   - Player.jsx already listens for the audio ended event and calls MusicContext.nextSong().
   - Repeat One loops the same track.
   - Repeat All wraps to the first/last track.
   - Shuffle selects another track when more than one track exists.

7. Previous
   - Pressing Previous after more than 3 seconds restarts the current track.
   - Pressing Previous within the first 3 seconds moves to the previous queue item.

8. Default volume
   - New audio starts at 100% volume and is unmuted.

No API URL was changed by this queue correction.
